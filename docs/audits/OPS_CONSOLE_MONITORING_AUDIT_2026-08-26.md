# Ops Console Audit — Monitoring & Management vs Industry Standard

**Date:** 2026-08-26 · **Scope:** full `apps/ops-console` (37 pages) + the `/ops/*` backend surface in `apps/auth-service` · **Method:** 3 parallel exploration agents (monitoring surfaces / management surfaces / platform quality), findings verified against source with file:line evidence.
**Prior audits:** OPS_CONSOLE_AUDIT_2026-07-06 (70/100), WEBAPP_DATA_COVERAGE_AUDIT_2026-07-07 (61→~82), OPS_CONSOLE_AUDIT_2026-08-07 (68/100 → all 54 fixed).

## Verdict

| Axis           | Score       | One-liner                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Managing**   | **82/100**  | At or above industry standard for an internal ops console at this stage. Deep workflows, real server-side RBAC, broad audit trail, strong session security.                                                               |
| **Monitoring** | **58/100**  | NOT yet a control-room product. It is a _watched-tab_ console: everything polls, a hidden tab goes blind, idle logout disarms the SOS bar, alerting is SOS-only, and there is zero platform-health or self-observability. |
| **Overall**    | **~70/100** | Industry standard to manage; below standard to monitor.                                                                                                                                                                   |

What "industry standard" means for a protection-company control console: push/websocket real-time to the operator, audible + browser-push alerting for every critical event class (not just one), a service-health surface, error tracking on the console itself, complete audit coverage of money-and-access mutations, and at least smoke-level test/CI coverage. Pre-fix, the console cleared none of the monitoring bar and most of the management bar.

---

## A. What is genuinely strong (keep)

- **SOS alerting is real and well-built.** Console-wide persistent bar on every authed page (`src/components/SosAlertBar.tsx`, mounted `Shell.tsx:434`), Web-Audio two-tone alarm re-sounding every 20 s until ack, autoplay gate handled honestly ("SOUND OFF — ENABLE"), tab-title mutation `(N) SOS`, `aria-live="assertive"`, full ack→escalate→resolve workflow on `/sos`.
- **Mission-detail staleness is best-practice.** `/live/[id]` derives fix age with clock-skew correction, 90 s STALE / 300 s LOST SIGNAL, 15 s local tick so a dead feed still re-renders (`live/[id]/page.tsx:321-361`). `/protection` uses server-clock `live/delayed/unavailable/idle` states.
- **Dispatch observability.** `/dispatch` shows the full offer cascade (rank, distance, status, live expiry countdown) + admin-gated killswitch; `/dispatch-inspector/[id]` gives a forensic event timeline.
- **Session security is unusually strong.** httpOnly cookie pair + CSRF double-submit, silent refresh 60 s before expiry, 15-min idle logout, revocation semantics hard-won from B-71 (a bare RBAC 403 does not boot you), logout wipes the messenger IDB vault and hard-navigates (`Shell.tsx:216-313`, `lib/api.ts:44-100`). CSP with per-request nonce.
- **Server RBAC is the real boundary.** `JwtAuthGuard + CsrfGuard + AdminGuard` on every ops controller; `@RequireRoles` on nearly all mutations; region scoping; ADMIN-only GDPR erase; last-ADMIN demotion guard + session revoke on role change.
- **Audit trail is broad with a fail-closed critical set** (booking/agent/mission/sos/wallet/dispute/dispatch actions), cursor-paginated viewer with CSV, per-subject timelines, separately-audited PII reveal.
- **CSV formula-injection guard** (`lib/csv.ts`) is correct OWASP mitigation.
- Honest de-faking culture: fake sparklines, fake ⌘K, fake settings form all removed with tombstone comments.
